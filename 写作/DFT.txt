All calculations were carried out utilizing the density functional theory (DFT) methodology. The exchange and correction interaction were described by the PerdewBurke-Ernzerhof (PBE) functional within the generalized gradient approximation. The all DFT computations utilized a centered Monkhorst-Pack scheme with a K-point resolved value of 777 Å-1 and the cutoff energy of plane wave basis sets was set to 600 eV for all structure optimizations. The convergence threshold for structure optimization was set to be reached when the force on each atom in the structure fell below 0.05 eV Å-1 and the energy convergence criterion was set to 1×10-5 eV for self-consistent field calculations. The energies of all molecules were calculated within a 60×60×60 Å box. For the surface and adsorption configurations, a 35 Å vacuum layer were introduced in order to avoid any spurious interactions between periodic images of the system and the bottom three layers were fixed to mimic the bulk structure. Calculation involves the definition of the adsorption energy using this equation:
Eads = Eads_config – E1 – E2
Where the Eads_config is the DFT energy of adsorption configuration, E1 and E2 represent the corresponding adsorption molecule, and the corresponding surface.
